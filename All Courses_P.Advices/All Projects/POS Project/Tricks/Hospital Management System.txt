4. Hospital Management System
Description:
A desktop application to manage patient records, appointments, and medical history for a hospital or clinic.

Features:
Patient Management: Add, update, and delete patient records (e.g., name, age, contact details).

Appointment Scheduling: Schedule and manage appointments with doctors.

Medical History: Record and view patient medical history.

Billing System: Generate and manage patient bills.

Reporting: Generate reports for appointments, patient history, and revenue.

User Authentication: Login system for doctors, nurses, and administrators.

Technologies:
.NET Framework: Windows Forms for the UI.

SQL Server: Database to store patient, appointment, and billing data.

Entity Framework: ORM for database interactions.
